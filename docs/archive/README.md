# Superseded proposals and document lineage

**Deprecated on 17 October 2026. Not an active research workflow.**

AstroTrust 2.0 discontinued the proposed public participant survey to focus on
system behavior evaluation. The old proposal linked model responses to future
human trust/reliance experiments; the revised project makes no such empirical
claims. No questionnaire, consent form, recruitment tooling or participant responses
were found in the repository. The survey scope statement was the only survey asset.

`pre-2.0/` contains exact pre-pivot bytes, including the existing uncommitted
consumer-collector additions to README. Original paths are encoded with `__` and
suffixed `.txt` to preserve Markdown as source without presenting outdated
instructions or relative links as active navigation. For example,
`docs__methodology.md.txt` preserves `docs/methodology.md`;
`survey__README.md.txt` preserves the discontinued survey proposal.

Archived documents: root README; methodology, ethics, benchmark card, scale-up gate,
instrument version, annotation handbook; data, analysis, experiments and survey READMEs.
Original relative links describe their original repository locations. Foundation,
instrument, first-snapshot and collector verification audits elsewhere in `docs/`
remain dated historical records. Their test counts and readiness descriptions are
observations of those milestones, not current states. No manuscript file was present;
the old working-paper title is preserved in the archived README.

Current authority: [AstroTrust 2.0 plan](../ASTROTRUST-2.0-PLAN.md) and
[pivot audit](../ASTROTRUST-PIVOT-AUDIT.md). Frozen instruments, reviewer packets,
raw captures and original exports remain at their original paths.
