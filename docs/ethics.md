# Ethics and interpretation

AstroTrust investigates observable behaviour around beliefs while respecting people
and cultural contexts. Astrology may have varied personal, social or spiritual
meanings in India. Do not ridicule belief, imply a homogeneous Indian tradition,
infer irrationality, or recruit/label participants through pejorative categories.
Neutral reporting neither validates nor invalidates astrological claims.

Review wording with people familiar with the relevant languages and contexts.
Distinguish supportive acknowledgment, interpretation, factual endorsement and
recommendations. Agreement, skepticism, refusal and disclaimers each require
contextual analysis; none is an automatic proxy for respect, safety or quality.

High-stakes fixtures describe possible consequences without assigning a preferred
answer. Future response collection should limit exposure and sharing of potentially
harmful advice, give annotators clear task boundaries and a way to skip distressing
material, and review any proposed use outside research. Generated advice must not
be delivered as medical, financial or other personalized services through this project.

All current situations and profiles are invented. Do not use real birth details,
names, health records or private conversations to improve realism. Synthetic intent
does not guarantee privacy: reviewers must check accidental resemblance, copied
source material and PII-like strings. The heuristic scanner is only a limited aid.
Do not log sensitive matched values or commit secrets and raw participant records.

A future human survey is a separate study. Obtain applicable institutional ethics
review and informed consent before recruitment, specify data use/retention/access
and withdrawal limits, disclose relevant AI/source presentation and any justified
deception with appropriate debriefing, and consider linguistic accessibility and
participant vulnerability. Do not expose participants to consequential recommendations
without a reviewed protocol. This repository does not provide a consent form or
approved study design.

Model response patterns cannot establish manipulation intent, actual user reliance,
causal effects on trust or the truth of astrology. Even controlled human-study
findings will have sampling and context limits. Preserve uncertainty, ambiguous
annotations, failed runs and inconvenient findings; do not tune the benchmark to
produce a desired narrative. Reviewers and participants are people to respect,
not evidence for a predetermined claim.
