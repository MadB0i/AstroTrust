\section{Limitations, Scope Boundaries, and Policy Proposals}
\label{sec:policy}

\paragraph{Methodological Limits.}
Our findings carry four clear boundaries. First, our live consumer audit covers $N=6$ sessions across 5 Indian apps, and single-researcher deterministic inspection plus non-circular manual auditing (\nolinkurl{data/astroguard/heldout_unseen_labeled.jsonl}) await independent multi-rater adjudication with Cohen's $\kappa$; we provide a masked 40-unit second-coder packet (\nolinkurl{tools/export_blind_coding_batch.py}) compatible with our repository's $\kappa$ CLI (\texttt{astrotrust annotations agreement}). Second, while our local evaluation now spans all 8 domains $\times 3$ belief frames across two parameter scales ($N=60$, enabling genuine out-of-domain testing), our frontier web ($N=27$) and live consumer ($N=6$) runs remain centered on \texttt{atb-career-001}. Third, live proxy capture on an Android~14 emulator confirmed unauthenticated execution of \texttt{POST save/user-upi-apps}, whereas \texttt{POST user/apps/installed} awaits an authenticated test session, and server-side dynamic pricing logic remains outside client-side observability. Fourth, we evaluated static binary forensics across all five live-audited APKs (\texttt{Astrotalk}, \texttt{Pandit Ji}, \texttt{Reva}, \texttt{AstroTime}, and \texttt{AstroSage AI} \texttt{v32.6}); broader Play Store sampling across additional vendors remains future work.

\newpage
\paragraph{Governance Recommendations for India.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt,topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Close the Android \texttt{<queries>} Loophole:} App stores should apply \texttt{QUERY\_ALL\_PACKAGES}-equivalent policy review whenever an app declares large enumerated \texttt{<queries>} blocks (e.g., $>50$ unrelated consumer packages), preventing vulnerability catalogs like \texttt{Astrotalk}'s 787-app list from bypassing Play visibility safeguards.
\item \textbf{Enforce DPDP Act~2023 Purpose Limitation and Data Minimization:} Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023~\citep{dpdp2023}, personal data processing must be limited to what is strictly necessary for a specified purpose; probing 787 unrelated banking, lending, gambling, reproductive-health, and welfare packages---and transmitting UPI app lists prior to login---exceeds any legitimate horoscope-delivery purpose.
\item \textbf{Enforce CCPA Rules on Nominal-Fee UPI Mandates:} Offering an introductory INR~1 consultation screen that registers a multi-year INR~699 recurring UPI Autopay mandate warrants direct scrutiny under India's CCPA \emph{Subscription Trap} and \emph{Bait and Switch} provisions, alongside upfront per-minute rate locks to stop post-session surges stranding wallet balances.
\item \textbf{Restrict Anti-Audit \texttt{FLAG\_SECURE} Blocks:} Operating systems and consumer regulators should limit screenshot blocking (\texttt{FLAG\_SECURE}) to OTP, PIN, and payment-entry views rather than AI consultation transcripts so users and auditors can document financial or medical claims made inside paid chats.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Research Ethics and Observational Scope.}
All prompts and consumer app interactions relied exclusively on synthetic scenarios and fictional personas (\texttt{consumer-profile-001} and fictional chat inputs), with zero real-user data, participant recruitment, or human-subject records. Consumer billing verification used minimal single-session test amounts (INR~1 and INR~11.81) with immediate UPI Autopay mandate revocation after checkout capture. Binary auditing inspected publicly distributed APKs strictly via non-destructive static parsing, and dynamic proxy capture ran on an isolated researcher-controlled Android~14 emulator without probing or modifying external backend systems. All observations document publicly observable client behavior, manifest declarations, and checkout flows captured at the stated package versions and dates for independent academic audit and public-interest transparency; app behaviors may evolve in subsequent releases, and verifiable vendor corrections or updated builds can be appended to the public record.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across $N=93$ logged interactions, five disassembled Android binaries, and live Android~14 proxy telemetry, our audit demonstrates a stark dissociation: \emph{surface epistemic calibration does not guarantee consumer product safety}. General-purpose frontier models uphold non-empirical boundaries without monetization hooks (27/27), while unmonetized small open-weight models exhibit scale-dependent epistemic instability (76.7\% at 0.5B vs.\ 53.3\% at 1.5B). Indian consumer AI astrology apps, by contrast, pair calibrated text disclaimers (3/6 sessions) with 100\% structural dark patterns (6/6 sessions)---including five-year UPI Autopay traps behind INR~1 trials, post-session rate surges, and cross-app audio/identity clones---while \texttt{Astrotalk}'s 787-app \texttt{<queries>} catalog and pre-login \texttt{save/user-upi-apps} telemetry operate completely outside text-only checks. Securing high-stakes consumer AI therefore requires paired text and binary/wire auditing (\texttt{Rules-v2} plus \texttt{AstroAudit}) alongside targeted enforcement under India's CCPA~2023 and DPDP~2023 frameworks.
